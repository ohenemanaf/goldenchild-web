Junior High School

Ages: 12 - 14 years

An advanced curriculum preparing students for higher education with specialized subjects, research projects, and leadership opportunities. In JHS 3, students sit the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

Subjects in the GES junior high curriculum

  • {{icon:book-marked}}English Language
  • {{icon:languages}}Ghanaian Language
  • {{icon:calculator}}Mathematics
  • {{icon:flask-conical}}Science
  • {{icon:globe}}Social Studies
  • {{icon:church}}Religious and Moral Education
  • {{icon:ruler}}Career Technology
  • {{icon:pen-tool}}Creative Arts and Design
  • {{icon:laptop}}Computing
  • {{icon:languages}}French
  • {{icon:dumbbell}}Physical and Health Education

{{icon:map-pin}} JHS 1 to 3 are taught at the First Stop campus.

Subjects follow the Ghana Education Service standards-based curriculum. Ask the school office about the subjects offered at each campus.
