Welcome to Golden Child Complex School

Established in 1999, Golden Child Complex School has been a beacon of educational excellence in Ghana. We offer comprehensive education from Creche to Junior High School, focusing on academic excellence, character development, and nurturing each child's unique talents.

What began at a single campus in James Town has grown into one school family across four campuses, so more children can learn close to home.

Our Story

How Golden Child grew, one campus at a time.

  1. 1999

    James Town, our first campus

    Golden Child School opens at James Town, the storied metropolis of the school, teaching Creche to Primary 3.

  2. Second campus

    First Stop

    First Stop becomes the perpetual heartbeat of Golden Child School, home to the majority of our students and the campus that teaches all the way to JHS 3.

  3. 2008

    Abaase

    Our Proprietor, Dr. Evans, builds the third campus at Abaase, teaching Creche to Primary 3.

  4. Fourth campus

    Kasoa-Ofankor

    The fourth campus extends quality education further, again from Creche to Primary 3.
