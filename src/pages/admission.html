Admission Process

Four steps from first enquiry to your child's first day.

  1. 1

    Application

    Complete and submit the application form online or in person at our administrative office.

  2. 2

    Assessment

    Schedule an assessment test and interview to determine the appropriate grade level placement.

  3. 3

    Acceptance

    Receive admission decision within two weeks of completing the assessment process.

  4. 4

    Enrollment

    Complete registration by paying fees and submitting required documents to secure your child's place.

Questions & Answers

Quick answers for parents and guardians.

What ages do you admit?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Creche takes children from 6 months to 2 years, Kindergarten 3 to 5 years, Primary School 6 to 11 years and Junior High School 12 to 14 years.

Which campus teaches Junior High School?{{icon:chevron-down}}

JHS 1 to 3, and Primary 4 to 6, are taught at the First Stop campus. James Town, Abaase and Kasoa-Ofankor teach Creche to Primary 3. See all campuses.

What documents do I need?{{icon:chevron-down}}

A completed application form, the birth certificate (original and photocopy), immunization records, four recent passport-sized photographs, previous school records for transfers, and the parent or guardian's ID (original and photocopy).

How do I get an application form?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Collect one at our administrative office, or email us to request one.

How soon will I hear back?{{icon:chevron-down}}

You receive the admission decision within two weeks of completing the assessment.

What do the fees cover?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Fees include tuition, books, and basic supplies. Additional costs may apply for uniforms, transportation, and extracurricular activities. See the fee structure.

Can I pay in instalments?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Yes. Payment plans are available, and there is a 5% discount for paying the full annual fee.

When is the office open?{{icon:chevron-down}}

Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
